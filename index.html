Questions about the virtual anatomy lab

What can I explore in this 3D anatomy laboratory?

Explore selectable organs, muscles, bones, nerves, blood vessels, and reproductive structures. Use system opacity controls, body-region views, search, and structure cards to study their relationships.

How do the anatomy animations work?

Play illustrative breathing, heart contraction, digestive transit, urine transport, and nerve signal animations. Pause, restart, step through a stage, or adjust the playback speed.

Can I use the anatomy lab on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Drag with one finger to rotate the model, pinch to zoom, and use two fingers to pan. On a desktop, use the mouse or keyboard controls. A compatibility renderer is available when WebGL cannot be used.

Is this a medically validated anatomy atlas?

No. The models and motion are educational illustrations with simplified geometry. They have not been validated for formal medical instruction, assessment, diagnosis, or patient care. Use the included references and qualified instruction for further study.

Continue your study with OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e. Core fact summaries in the lab reference this textbook. Source checking does not validate the procedural models.
