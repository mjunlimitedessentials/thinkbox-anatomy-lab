Anatomy Laboratory — delivery and requirements checklist
Educational prototype. Original procedural geometry; not a medical-school anatomical atlas. Built without Blender, paid assets, patient data, external accounts, or API keys.

Implemented

  • 111 selectable structure groups, composed of 408 meshes (approximately 262,000 triangles).
  • 15 system controls, including reproductive options; organ and gland entries are grouped under their respective systems.
  • 360° rotation, zoom, pan, six directional views, region presets, picking, highlighting, search, focus, isolate, hide, reveal, and transparency.
  • System opacity, labels with basic overlap suppression, three-layer removal and restoration, exploded view, reset, full screen, help, and a colorblind-oriented palette.
  • Browser-local saved views including camera, anatomy option, opacity, hidden structures, cut plane, and exploded offset. No account or cloud progress storage.
  • Sagittal, coronal, axial, and oblique geometric clipping, with a movable position slider and reversed-side option. No capped tissue slices. Compatibility rendering uses approximate face-level clipping and does not draw the plane helper.
  • Five schematic animations: breathing/diaphragm motion, whole-heart pulse, intestinal transit marker, ureter transit marker, and sciatic nerve signal marker. Play, pause, speed, restart, and stepping.
  • Explore, Identify, Locate, and Clinical Challenge. Thirteen sourced quiz topics, three difficulty subsets for Identify/Locate, system and region filters, randomized order/options, feedback, session score, reset, and missed-question review.
  • Eight guided regional layer studies. These are visibility sequences, not realistic surgical dissections.

Systems and regions

Systems: skin/external envelope; representative muscles; skeleton; selected joints, discs, ligament, and tendon; brain/spinal cord; selected peripheral nerves; heart; major vessel pathways; respiratory; digestive; endocrine; urinary; lymphatic/immune; female reproductive; male reproductive.

Regions: whole body, head/neck, thorax, abdomen, pelvis, back, upper limb, lower limb, brain, spinal cord. Perineal detail, individual cranial nerves, and a full spinal root map are absent.

Incomplete or not implemented

Requested capabilityActual scope / remaining work
High-resolution anatomical accuracyNot achieved. Primitive-based shapes approximate relationships. No scanned or educator-validated mesh assets, textures, variants, or detailed attachment footprints.
Complete male/female anatomyInternal reproductive examples switch; external body envelope is shared. No comprehensive external genital anatomy or sex-specific skeletal proportions.
All muscles, joints, and nervesOnly representative structures. No full deep-muscle set, detailed cartilage, cranial nerves, or complete peripheral neurovascular networks.
Comprehensive information cardsCore cards have source-linked facts. Many catalog entries are partial. Missing drainage, supply, innervation, origin/insertion, and clinical facts are explicitly left unprovided.
Sectional CT / MRI comparisonAbsent. Open mesh cutaways are not diagnostic sections. The slider moves a cutting plane; direct dragging of the plane itself is not implemented.
Detailed physiologyNo separate cardiac chamber/valve timing, full blood-flow circuit, nephron filtration, CSF circulation, or cellular muscle contraction.
Joint movementsNo articulated flexion, extension, rotation, pronation, supination, or ankle movement simulator.
Disease demonstrationsShort educational clinical questions only. No visual pathology simulation for the 15 requested conditions.
Advanced assessmentSmall demonstration bank, not a validated exam. Clinical cases share the filtered bank across difficulty levels; no adaptive assessment or longitudinal analytics.
High-detail performance optimizationOne locally bundled procedural model; no progressive region loading or level-of-detail meshes. Device-specific performance requires further testing.

Medical evidence and expert review

Core fact cards and quiz topics were checked against relevant OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e sections. Links are in Help & references and applicable cards. This does not validate spatial geometry or establish clinical accuracy. A qualified anatomy educator or physician must review all model positions, topology, attachments, reproductive anatomy, clinical cases, nomenclature, and assessment answers before formal use. Detailed terminological compliance has not been certified.

This is not a diagnostic tool and does not replace clinical training, professional judgment, cadaveric study, radiology instruction, or patient care experience.

Controls and launch

Open the delivered website in a WebGL-capable modern browser. No installation is required. Drag rotates, wheel/pinch zooms, right/Shift-drag or two-finger drag pans. Search or click selects; Focus centers a selection; Reset restores the start view. Keyboard support: arrow-key rotation, Shift+up/down pan, +/− zoom, R whole-body view. Touch controls are provided by OrbitControls.

Validation

Eleven automated checks passed: unique IDs/names, coverage of every system control, finite geometry, valid quiz targets, difficulty filtering, system/region filtering and empty combinations, single-submission scoring, shuffle integrity, normalized/reversible clipping planes, anatomical left/right and renal asymmetry conventions, and local entrypoint/import availability. JavaScript syntax checks passed for all authored modules. Desktop Chrome browser checks passed for rendering through the compatibility renderer; search and fact panels; isolate/context, hide/reveal, transparency and reset; camera presets; correct and incorrect quiz answers; score accumulation and restart; clinical questions; direct model picking in Locate; section selection, plane slider and reversal; animation play/pause/step; male/female switch; save/restore view; layer removal/restoration; guided-study steps; help and report opening. The browser displayed a score of 2/3 after one wrong and two correct answers, and 0/0 after restart. High-quality WebGL rendering could not be tested because the test browser disables WebGL. Mobile layout, physical touch/trackpad gestures, all directional presets, full-screen transitions, and cross-browser performance remain unverified. No formal FPS benchmark or medical validation was performed.

Compatibility and next steps

Targets modern Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari with ES modules. WebGL2 provides the primary renderer; a reduced-detail Canvas renderer projects the same 3D scene when WebGL is unavailable. Only browser combinations actually tested are claimed in the validation section. Rendering uses capped pixel density and modest-resolution mesh primitives. Browser-local views may be unavailable in restricted/private storage environments. No external asset calls are required during use.

Recommended next version: integrate legally licensed, medically reviewed anatomical meshes; add precise internal anatomy, validated movement rigs, capped sections and licensed radiology comparisons; expand and independently review the assessment bank; then complete multi-device accessibility, performance, and medical validation.
